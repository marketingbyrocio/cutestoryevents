]]> 2024 was a year full of firsts, amazing joy, and cute community connections. I’m so excited to look back on some of the 14+ events Cute Story Events brought to life last year! Let’s take a trip down memory lane to celebrate what we accomplished together.

Tet Vietnamese Lunar New Year

January kicked off with the Tết Vietnamese Lunar New Year Celebration, a breathtaking festival that welcomed the Year of the Dragon with stunning traditional performances, delicious food, and heartwarming community spirit. From lion dances to vibrant fashion and traditional performances, every detail reflected the beauty of Vietnamese culture and tradition.

First Black History Month in Fircrest

In February, Reclaiming Us, Reciprocity Consulting, and local neighbors made history with the first-ever Black History Month Celebration in Fircrest. The day was alive with delectable food, fresh juice, kid activities and community voices that came together to celebrate heritage and inspire connection. It was an unforgettable moment of pride and joy for all involved! Photos are by the lovely Merissa Humes.

Galentines with Supper Sociale

February brought all the love vibes with the first annual Galentines, hosted by Supper Sociale. This cozy yet chic gathering celebrated friendship and community over an exquisite meal, creative cocktails, and interactive activities. It was an evening full of laughter, connection, and new memories.

Copperbox Core Purpose Wander Event Activation at SXSW 

In March, at SXSW, Copperbox delivered an exclusive experience that offered a refreshing break from the hustle and bustle. Guests were immersed in a nature setting that encouraged meaningful connections and deeper reflection. Elite networking opportunities brought together high-level executives to tackle challenges and foster collaborations that will shape the future. Personalized coaching sessions and insightful reflection activities helped participants gain clarity on their strengths and growth areas, making this activation both impactful and inspiring.

Marketing Conference in Downtown LA

Later in March, we blended a bit of business and fun at a marketing conference in LA. This lively event featured engaging talks from top brands, innovative activations (including Shark Tank alumni!), and countless networking opportunities. Guests enjoyed a catered lunch, signature cocktails, and roundtable discussions that inspired new ideas and connections.

Sculpt Bodywork Open House Event

May brought the perfect balance of calm and celebration with the Sculpt Bodywork Open House Event. This dreamy day of self-care was packed with rejuvenating activities, wellness demos, and thoughtful healthy snacks and drinks. Guests also participated in raffles to win self-care services and products, leaving them refreshed, inspired, and glowing from the inside out.

Broken Bone Bathtub: The Documentary Screening

In July + August, Cute Story Events teamed up with the incredible performance artist Siobhán O’Loughlin for two unforgettable screenings of Broken Bone Bathtub. The film captured Siobhán’s years-long immersive tour in bathtubs across the globe, exploring human connection in the most intimate of spaces. Guests were treated to a photo opp, an adorable merch booth, and a heartfelt talkback with Siobhán herself, making the event as interactive and inspiring as the film. Held in both Tacoma and Seattle, it was a celebration of art, vulnerability, and the power of live experiences.

Summer in the City: NYC Marketing Event

July turned up the heat with another marketing event in New York. We brought together over 700 marketing professionals for an engaging conference packed with insights and networking opportunities. Attendees explored trending topics like TikTok marketing, community building, and subscription and loyalty programs through panels and workshops hosted by industry experts from leading brands. Hands-on sessions covered practical strategies, while a lively happy hour in the kiosk area sparked meaningful connections. After months of planning, it was thrilling to see this event come to life and foster collaboration and growth in the direct to consumer marketing space.
